BookCycleBD — an online second-hand book marketplace

A peer-to-peer marketplace built to make second-hand books more affordable and accessible in Bangladesh. Users publish structured listings with images, search and filter on book-specific criteria, and contact sellers directly; an administrative panel handles users, listings, categories and reports. Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Supabase PostgreSQL, NextAuth.js, Cloudinary and Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel.
